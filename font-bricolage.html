Questions

Are you available for new work?

Yes. I’m open to full-time roles and to freelance projects. The quickest way to reach me is by email or on LinkedIn.

Do you build sites in Arabic?

Yes. Six of the projects above run in English and Arabic. I set up WPML or Polylang, build the right-to-left layouts, and check typography, forms and menus in both languages.

Yes. I can audit it, fix what is broken, speed it up and keep it updated, whether it was built with a custom theme or with Elementor.

Which tools do you use?

WordPress, Elementor, WPML, PHP, MySQL, React, Next.js, TypeScript, SCSS, GSAP, Webflow, Drupal, Git and Figma.

Dubai, UAE (GMT+4). I work on site with teams in Dubai and remotely with teams elsewhere.
